# Namibia Quest Gameplay Bible

Working design document for the full Namibia Quest campaign. This document captures the agreed direction after the Namibia trip review and should guide region-by-region implementation.

## Core Direction

Namibia Quest is a premium mobile-first expedition adventure built around the real 2026 Namibia route. The game should feel like a family travel journal, a light RPG, a modern board game, and a collection of varied mini-game styles.

The key principle: every region must give the player agency. The player should not only look at scenes or collect photos. They should act, decide, move, plan, negotiate, explore, balance, flee, climb, craft, or solve problems.

Photography can still exist as a reward, chronicle entry, or discovery confirmation, but it should not be the primary mechanic unless paired with meaningful active gameplay.

## Whole-Game RPG Layer

### Expedition Profile

The game begins in Windhoek by preparing both the 4x4 and the player character. Early equipment choices create a light RPG profile that influences later mini-games without making any activity impossible.

The system should feel like a board-game balance layer: real consequences, soft modifiers, no fun-killing dead builds.

### Hero Profiles

Before choosing equipment, the player chooses a humorous expedition avatar. This character becomes the player's visible companion across the whole game: portrait in profile screens, small map/profile medallion, and later possible dialogue flavor.

Current hero set:

- Kapitan 4x4: exaggerated muscular road captain, calm driving, good for 4x4, road events, and pressure behind the wheel.
- Tropicielka Szczegolow: sharp observer, excellent for tracks, animals, clues, and safari signals.
- Mistrz Logistyki: rounder overprepared planner, strong in packing, resources, route planning, and camp setup.
- Wytrwaly Wedrowiec: physical hiker, strong in dunes, rocks, long walks, and stamina tasks.
- Kronikarka Szlaku: memory/story specialist, strong in history, culture, dialogue, and final chronicle.
- Ryzykant Adrenaliny: speed-focused adventurer, strong in quads, sandboarding, chases, and quick reactions.
- Negocjator Opowiesci: talkative, chaotic, funny social character; weak organization, very strong in bargaining and human encounters.
- Safari Diva: stylish hotel-and-pool traveler accidentally on safari; weak in rough survival, strong socially and under civilized pressure.

Design rule: hero choice provides base stats and personality. Equipment then modifies those stats. The result should create different builds without locking the player out of any region.

Visual rule: hero portraits should be more distinct than realistic travel portraits. Use light Jumanji-like exaggeration: recognizable silhouettes, different dominant colors, visible class fantasy, and humor, while keeping premium family adventure quality.

### Proposed Stats

- Kondycja: stamina, climbing, hiking, long physical tasks.
- Szybkosc: running, chase sequences, fast reactions.
- Odpornosc: heat, fatigue, long desert exposure.
- Uwaznosc: tracks, details, animal behavior, conversations, clues.
- Organizacja: packing, planning, resource management, strategy boards.
- Spokoj: animal encounters, negotiation, pressure decisions, respectful dialogue.

### Example Equipment Tradeoffs

- More water: higher Odpornosc, lower Szybkosc.
- Light pack: higher Szybkosc, lower safety margin in heat.
- Hat: better sun protection and slower heat gain.
- Trekking boots: better stability on rocks and dunes, slightly slower movement.
- Binoculars or notebook: stronger Uwaznosc, but takes space.

### Balance Rule

Stats should change comfort, margins, and optimal choices. They should not lock the player out of content. Each mini-game remains playable with any profile.

## Region Plans

### 1. Windhoek - Start Wyprawy

Guardian: Straznik Wyprawy.

Tone: logistics, first evening, first game steak, museum/history, city edge baboons.

Main game: Expedition Prep.

Mechanic:

- Inventory/tetris packing of the 4x4.
- Choose water, clothing, tools, food, camping gear.
- Keep important items accessible.
- Weight and space influence the Expedition Profile.

Supporting activities:

- First Game Steak: short restaurant scene and food memory.
- Museum / Historia Namibii: timeline puzzle about colonialism, independence, and modern Namibia.
- Baboons at the Edge: road-exit event about slowing down, securing food, and respecting wildlife.

Visual style: premium travel journal, 4x4 prep, warm first-day city atmosphere.

Reward: expedition profile, first chronicle page, readiness for the route.

### Route Encounter - Solitaire Apple Pie Outpost

Tone: strange roadside postapocalyptic charm, rusted cars, old petrol station, phone booth remains, ground squirrels, legendary apple pie.

Main game: Roadside Maze.

Mechanic:

- Top-down or isometric exploration among rusted wrecks and sandy paths.
- Small maze-like layout.
- Move gates, follow clues, avoid rusty dead ends.
- Ground squirrels reveal shortcuts by running into burrows.
- Final objective: find the apple pie.

Visual style: sun-bleached roadside Americana/postapo desert, spooky but safe.

Reward: Legendary Solitaire Apple Pie chronicle collectible and energy before Sesriem.

### 2. Sesriem - Brama Pustyni

Guardian: Oryks - Straznik Pustyni.

Tone: desert base, sunset dune, morning fog, canyon, first self-made braai. Survival-lite but family-friendly.

Main loop:

- Rozbij Camp: choose shade, wind protection, tent placement, water access.
- First Braai: manage fire, heat, timing, food.
- Sunset Dune Walk: stamina and balance before sunset.
- Sesriem Canyon: route planning through shade, heat, and limited water.
- Morning Fog Departure: find gear and pack camp in low visibility.

Mechanics:

- Water and heat as soft survival resources.
- Camp setup affects later ease.
- Wind, sun, and shade matter.

Visual style: warm camp colors, orange dunes, cool fog morning contrast.

Reward: first true desert chronicle entry and oryx guardian connection.

### 3. Big Daddy / Deadvlei - Gigant Wydm

Guardian: Gigant Wydm.

Tone: misty Deadvlei sunrise, dead trees, first sunlight on dunes, endless views, oryx on distant dunes.

Main game: Ridge Climb Hybrid.

Phases:

1. Deadvlei at Dawn: navigate mist, dead trees, pale clay pan, find start of dune route.
2. Big Daddy Climb: stamina, rhythm steps, balance, wind, route choices.
3. Dune Descent: fast satisfying descent after the climb.

Mechanics:

- Tap/hold rhythm for steps.
- Swipe to correct balance.
- Choose steep/short or long/easier route.
- Rest restores stamina but sun/heat rise.

Visual style: cinematic 2.5D desert, fog-to-sunrise transition, huge scale.

Reward: panoramic chronicle entry and dune guardian.

### 4. Swakopmund - Mgla Atlantyku i Zywa Pustynia

Guardian: Straznik Mgly Atlantyku.

Tone: foggy evening, stormy ocean, jetty restaurant among waves, gulls fighting wind, living desert, quads, sandboarding.

Main game: Living Desert Tracker.

Mechanic:

- Read tracks in the sand with a guide.
- Find hidden chameleons, small vipers, lizards.
- Choose track direction.
- Carefully brush sand aside.
- Wind slowly erases clues.

Adventure package: Dune Rush.

- Quad Dune Run: fast dune riding, jumps, landing balance.
- Sandboard Speed: high-speed dune descent, steering and balance.

Scene: Jetty in the Fog.

- Short balance/wind/f wave scene while crossing toward the restaurant.

Visual style: cold ocean fog contrasted with bright living desert sand.

Reward: Atlantic mist guardian and living desert chronicle entry.

### 5. Walvis Bay - Laguna Zycia

Guardian: Foka - Straznik Oceanu.

Current implementation exists as Seal Kayak Adventure and Wildlife Search Board 01. Future V2 should shift from obstacle survival toward active wildlife encounters.

Main game: Seal Kayak Encounter.

Mechanic:

- Steer kayak among moving seals and lagoon obstacles.
- Safe close seal encounters give points.
- Seals do not remove lives, but can cause balance/spin/slowdown events.
- Occasional Seal on Kayak balance event.
- Keep drift, steering, timer, score, and open lagoon movement.

Prologue package:

- Pelican Boat: pelican lands on boat, organize deck and keep calm.
- Dolphin Wake: keep boat course and tempo while dolphins appear nearby.

Wildlife Search:

- Keep as side/prototype activity, not the primary long-term mechanic.

Visual style: lively lagoon, playful wildlife, ocean blue, boats, seals, pelicans, dolphins.

Reward: Ocean Guardian Seal and Walvis Bay chronicle.

### 6. Sandwich Harbour - Dwa Swiaty

Guardian: Straznik Dwoch Swiatow.

Tone: 4x4 between ocean and dunes, rising tide, sand, waves, dune climbs, wild descents.

Main game: Tide Run 4x4.

Phases:

1. Tide Run: drive the shrinking corridor between waves and dunes before tide cuts the route.
2. Dune Climb: maintain speed, choose angle, avoid bogging down.
3. Crazy Descent: fast downhill arcade descent.

Mechanics:

- Steering, throttle, soft sand, wave pressure.
- Tide clock narrows safe path.
- Momentum matters on dunes.

Visual style: turquoise ocean left, gold dunes right, dynamic mobile vertical track.

Reward: two-worlds guardian and chronicle entry about the road that exists only briefly.

### Route Encounter - Skeleton Coast Shipwreck

Tone: dark rust, fog, waves, abandoned ship, cabins, broken floor, water entering hull, animals in shadows.

Preferred style: Doom/Quake-lite first-person exploration without combat.

Mechanic:

- Move through ship corridors and cabins.
- Open doors, collect materials, notes, equipment.
- Use flashlight, rope, old key, map fragments.
- Environmental hazards: collapsing floor, flooding passage, sudden animal movement.
- Make choices under pressure.

Implementation approach:

- V1 can be fake-3D first person or node-based oldschool crawler.
- Later upgrade could use simple 3D if production allows.

Visual style: moody, dark, rusty, cinematic, spooky but family-safe.

Reward: Skeleton Coast chronicle encounter.

### 7. Spitzkoppe - Skaly i Gwiazdy

Guardian: Gwiezdny Wedrowiec.

Tone: excellent campsite among rocks, rock paintings, sunset at the arch, incredible stars.

Main game: Arch Sunset Scramble.

Mechanic:

- Reach the arch before sunset.
- Choose rock paths, balance, manage stamina.
- Quick swipe/tap decisions and route choices.

Supporting activities:

- Camp Among Rocks: layout puzzle for camp placement among granite formations.
- Rock Art Trail: explore and interpret rock paintings.
- Star Wanderer: rotate star map, connect constellations, find the onward route.

Visual style: pink/orange granite, golden sunset, deep starry night.

Reward: star guardian and night-sky chronicle entry.

### 8. Twyfelfontein - Znaki Skal

Guardian: Gekon - Straznik Skal.

Tone: extreme heat, guide-led rock engravings, ostriches crossing, Burnt Mountain and Organ Pipes as background, evening Damaraland drive.

Main game: Heat Trail.

Mechanic:

- Terrain survival-lite route planning.
- Move through sun and shade to reach engravings.
- Manage water, heat, and time.
- Petroglyph interpretation appears as checkpoint puzzles, not the main mechanic.

Event: Ostrich Road Dash.

- Short dynamic vehicle reaction event with ostriches crossing chaotically.

Epilogue: Damaraland Evening Drive.

- Scenic route choice before Madisa Camp.

Visual style: shimmering heat, red rock, harsh shadows, dry terrain.

Reward: gecko guardian and rock-sign chronicle.

### Route Encounter - Madisa Camp Elephant Chase

Tone: unfenced camp, night, desert elephant enters campsite, pressure, flashlight, rocks, tents, vehicles.

Main game: Elephant Camp Chase.

Mechanic:

- Top-down or pseudo-3D night runner.
- Elephant follows and changes speed.
- Choose paths at forks.
- Avoid tents, tables, rocks, vehicles.
- Use flashlight with limited battery.
- Wrong choices create dead ends or shorten elephant distance.

Failure:

- Elephant catches/cuts off the player; restart from checkpoint.

Visual style: dark camp, warm flashlight cones, huge elephant silhouette.

Reward: Madisa Camp survival chronicle.

### 9. Himba / Damara - Straznik Historii

Guardian: Starszy Himba - Straznik Historii.

Tone: open villages, conversation, entering homes, stories, traditional work, sacred fire, craft and ritual.

Main game: Village Walk RPG.

Mechanic:

- Top-down RPG inspired by old Diablo-like exploration, but peaceful.
- Walk through village, talk to people, enter homes, complete tasks.
- Trust/respect meter based on listening, asking, helping, and waiting for permission.

Damara Skill Trials:

- Gather herbs.
- Shoot bow.
- Make necklace.
- Prepare ostrich egg container.
- Make tools.
- Start fire rhythmically.

Himba Ritual Tasks:

- Smoke cleansing.
- Prepare skin mixture.
- Hair preparation.
- Visit homes.
- Speak with shaman.
- Understand sacred fire and kraal as village center.

Visual style: warm human-centered RPG, respectful and detailed.

Reward: history guardian and cultural chronicle entry.

### 10. Etosha - Kraina Wodopojow

Guardian: Slon - Straznik Sawanny.

Tone: strategic safari, waterholes, dust roads, animals everywhere, roadblocks, lions in grass, night waterholes with elephants and rhinos.

Main game: Safari Route Planner.

Style: modern board game / expedition strategy board.

Mechanic:

- Map of waterholes connected by roads.
- Car as expedition token.
- Day split into rounds: morning, midday, evening, night.
- Move costs time/fuel/fatigue.
- At waterholes choose to wait, observe, reposition, or leave.
- Events as cards/scenes.

Layers:

- Time of day.
- Dust and visibility.
- Waterhole character.
- Patience.
- Animal behavior signals.

Event cards:

- Elephant Roadblock.
- Zebra / Springbok Crossing.
- Giraffe Alarm leading to lions.
- Lion Grass Walk.
- Dust Drive.
- Bathing Elephants.
- Night Rhino.
- Night Lion Call.

Visual style: deluxe safari board game, tokens, illustrated cards, field map.

Reward: waterhole chronicle and elephant guardian.

### 11. Mount Etjo - Rhino Distance

Guardian: Nosorozec - Straznik Ochrony.

Tone: private reserve, five-hour safari, very close white rhinos in daylight, massive calm presence.

Main game: Rhino Distance.

Style: cinematic decision / precision safari.

Mechanic:

- Drive slowly through reserve.
- Rhino appears near road or crosses in front.
- Stop, reverse, wait, turn off engine, keep distance.
- Avoid sudden movement.
- Read rhino behavior.

Stat influence:

- Spokoj gives larger decision margin.
- Uwaznosc reveals behavior cues earlier.
- Organizacja helps vehicle-position choices.

Visual style: low camera from vehicle, dust, huge white rhino scale.

Reward: conservation guardian and close-rhino chronicle.

### 12. Okahandja - Targ Karawan

Guardian: Kupiec Karawan.

Tone: craft market, souvenirs, humor, negotiation, short blanket economy, Monkey Island/Larry energy without losing respect.

Main game: Oldschool First-Person Trade RPG.

View:

- First-person stall/seller view.
- Bottom dialogue panel.
- Left/right/forward navigation between stalls.
- Inventory panel with budget, items, space, relationship mood.

Mechanic:

- Talk to sellers.
- Ask about object history.
- Make offers.
- Bundle items.
- Walk away and return.
- Sellers remember negotiation style.

Object system:

- Items have price, region theme, chronicle value, size, rarity, story, bargain flexibility.
- Souvenirs can softly compensate weaker region badges or enrich final ceremony.
- Limited budget and luggage space create strategic tradeoffs.

Seller personalities:

- Theatrical price master.
- Proud carver.
- Bundle seller.
- Storyteller.
- Last-chance negotiator.

Stat influence:

- Spokoj improves negotiation stability.
- Uwaznosc reveals hidden value or price clues.
- Organizacja helps budget and luggage planning.

Visual style: retro RPG interface, premium market art, humorous dialogue.

Reward: caravan merchant guardian, souvenirs, chronicle boosters.

### 13. Daan Viljoen - Wedrowiec Powrotu

Guardian: Wedrowiec Powrotu.

Tone: final walking safari, calm animals, baboons, wildebeest, springboks, last night, packing, returning the car.

Main game: Walking Safari.

Mechanic:

- Calm top-down/isometric walking route.
- Choose pace and path.
- Stop when animals cross.
- Avoid entering between baboons or herds.
- Respect distance without rushing.

Second activity: Last Pack.

- Emotional packing puzzle using souvenirs and items from the whole route.
- Fragile items must be protected.
- Objects trigger memories.
- Connects to Okahandja purchases.

Final scene: Return the Car.

- Checklist ritual: mileage, gear, final look at 4x4.

Visual style: quiet golden reserve, breathing space after intense regions.

Reward: return guardian and final pre-ceremony chronicle.

### 14. Ceremonia Straznikow - Krag Wyprawy

Guardian: Krag Straznikow.

Tone: final story assembly, not a boss fight. The ending is the journey becoming a chronicle.

Main game: Guardian Circle.

Mechanic:

- Arrange guardian seals in route order.
- Match memories, souvenirs, and chronicle pages to regions.
- Create a personal final map of meanings: desert, ocean, rocks, people, safari, road, return.
- Souvenirs can fill softer gaps in chronicle themes.

Second layer: Chronicle Assembly.

- Choose 5-7 defining moments.
- Arrange final page.
- Generate expedition title/flavor based on playstyle.

Possible final titles:

- Tropiciel Pustyni.
- Straznik Wodopojow.
- Mistrz Logistyki.
- Przyjaciel Fok.
- Wedrowiec Gwiazd.
- Kupiec z Okahandja.

Scoring philosophy:

- Avoid one harsh global score.
- Use themed badges: Pustynia, Ocean, Skaly, Kultura, Safari, Droga, Kronika.
- Badges can be partial/full or bronze/silver/gold.

Visual style: premium travel journal, warm light, map background, ceremonial badge circle.

Reward: Namibia Quest 2026 completed.

### Build Philosophy

Implement one region at a time after the design bible is stable. Avoid jumping between regions unless a shared system is required. Use each region to test one distinct gameplay style, then carry forward only the shared systems that are worth keeping.
